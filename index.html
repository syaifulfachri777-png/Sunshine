Want to know why every detail is deliberate?

Every accent in our bottles and labels is chosen the same way we choose ingredients — for what it does to the person holding it, not what looks good in a photo. Here's the story behind it.

Amber signals warmth before the wearer says a word. Mauve carries the same emotional register as rose absolute — intimacy, not sweetness. Sage settles a room the way vetiver settles a composition. Ink holds the others quiet, the way a good base note holds a fragrance together.
